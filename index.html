Services

Built to spec. Measured before it leaves.

Regrips, reshafts, swing weighting and full custom builds. Every club is measured on the bench and checked against its specs before it goes back in your bag.

01

Regripping

Old grips and tape fully stripped, then new grips installed straight and aligned on every club. Grip size is set with build-up tape, from standard to midsize and beyond, to fit your hands.

Bring your own grips, or we’ll order from Golf Pride, Lamkin, SuperStroke and others. Solvent-installed grips set overnight, so plan on next-day pickup.

02

Shaft Work

Steel and graphite shafts removed without damaging the head, using heat and a shaft puller on graphite. The hosel is cleaned, the new shaft is prepped and epoxied, and a fresh ferrule goes on.

Cut-downs and extensions are measured to an exact finished length, and swing weight is rechecked afterward. Epoxy cures overnight, so shaft work is ready the next day.

03

Swing Weighting

Swing weight is how heavy a club feels when you swing it, measured on a letter-and-number scale. Most men’s clubs fall around D0 to D3.

Every club is measured on a swingweight scale and built to the right target for its place in the bag. Clubs that fall outside the progression are brought back in line. Shafts, length and grip weight all change swing weight, so it’s checked on every job.

04

Custom Builds

Every build starts with a spec sheet: length, swing weight, grip size and shaft for each club, taken from your fitting or the manufacturer’s standard.

Heads, shafts and grips are dry-fit and checked before anything is glued, then measured against the sheet when finished. You get the build sheet with your clubs. Bring your own components or we’ll source them.

About specs: without a fitting, we generally build to the manufacturer’s specs. Have specs from a fitting? We’ll build to them exactly. Thinking about changing length, grip size or shafts? We’re happy to talk it through with you.

About

A small shop that does the work properly.

ForeUnder Golf USA is a home-based pro shop in Omaha. I’ve been building and repairing golf clubs for more than 30 years, starting long before big-box golf stores and online component shops.

The approach hasn’t changed: measure everything, build to spec, and treat every club like it’s going in my own bag. Whether it’s one grip or a full set, it gets the same attention.
